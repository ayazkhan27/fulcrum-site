- Biman: official fleet lists Boeing 787, 777, 737 and Dash 8-400. Do not imply a current Airbus fleet or unsupported dependence on captive passengers: https://www.biman-airlines.com/fleet
- Geography: South Asia, with connections toward Southeast Asia. Economic potential is expressed as Fulcrum’s belief, not a guaranteed forecast.
